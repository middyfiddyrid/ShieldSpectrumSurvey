## What's safe to say publicly vs. keep internal

**Safe to publish:**
- That Shield Security is DCS's first / launch partner.
- That DCS makes the sweep client-visible in real time and auto-generates an address-verified report.
- That DCS runs on accessible spectrum-analysis hardware (positioned on visibility + access).

**Do NOT publish (keep internal):**
- Any "we're the only ones who do X" claim. Competing professional TSCM software already does
  database classification and automated reporting (see the positioning brief). Public copy here
  stays framed against **"the old/manual way"** and **"six-figure kits"** — never against a named
  competitor, and never claiming sole capability. This protects against a false-advertising challenge.
- The specific hardware model, the underlying repo, Supabase/host details, pricing internals,
  or the "applied game theory / DeConfliction" thesis (that's internal framing, not buyer-facing).
- Any specific past client (Behrens, Grannemann, etc.) without that client's written consent.
