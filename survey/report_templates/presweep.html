{{ "5" if shielding else "4" }}.METHODOLOGY — WHAT THE SWEEP WILL DO

The premises are swept across the full frequency range the analyzer supports, with extra dwell time on the bands where surveillance devices commonly operate. An exterior reference reading lets us tell a signal that originates inside the premises from the ordinary signals present everywhere in the area.

Every detected signal is matched against the expected environment above and classified. Signals that cannot be accounted for are localized room-by-room and, where warranted, hunted with a directional antenna. The engagement includes Wi-Fi network hardening and cell-phone hardening. You receive a final report mirroring exactly what is found on site. This survey is receive-only; nothing is transmitted.

A.APPENDIX A — ALLOCATED-SPECTRUM MAP

The chart below is the frequency map the sweep is checked against: each colored band is a portion of the radio spectrum allocated to a known service at your location (0 Hz on the left to 6.1 GHz on the right). During the sweep, every detected emission is compared to this map — energy inside an expected band is attributed to its service and verified; energy that matches nothing here is treated as an anomaly and physically investigated.
