AMBIENT REFERENCE SWEEP outside / vehicle at curb — TSCM standard practice

Run one full sweep OUTSIDE the premises before sweeping interior rooms. The correlation engine compares interior vs. exterior amplitude per signal — a signal meaningfully stronger inside is locally sourced, even in a "plausible" band like 2.4 GHz.
