PHASE DIRECTION FINDING B220 two-antenna bearing — coherent RX-A + RX-B

    
Unlike the amplitude "warmer/colder" hunt above, this reads the
      phase difference of the wavefront across two antennas and computes an actual
      bearing. Needs the B220 (two RX chains, shared LO) and both antennas on a bar a
      known distance apart.
      Two-element arrays have a front/back mirror — the true bearing and its mirror are
      both shown; rotate the bar to break the tie (the true one stays put).

ACTIVE PROBE (PING) transmit a stimulus, find what reacts — needs a 2TX/2RX radio

    

      ⚠ This TRANSMITS. It only finds devices that REACT to a ping (receivers,
      oscillators, junctions) — a "no response" is not a clean bill; run it
      alongside the passive sweep, never instead of it. Simultaneous stimulate-and-listen
      needs the B220 (2TX/2RX); it is bench-pending, not yet field-validated.
      Stay in bands you are authorized to transmit in (ISM 902–928 / 2400–2483.5 / 5725–5875 MHz).

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
    

    

  

  

    
PASSIVE EMANATION SCAN find the SILENT bugs by the RF they leak — no transmitting

    
Catches a covert camera/recorder that never transmits and won't react to a
      ping: its clock still leaks a comb of evenly-spaced tones. Needs an SDR (Pluto/B220),
      a close antenna, and a relatively quiet spot. A null result is one independent look, not
      a clean bill — run it alongside the sweep and the active probe.

    NETWORK CHECK who is on the client's WiFi / LAN
    
  
    
    
    Lists every device on this laptop's own network — IP, MAC,
    vendor, and hostname — by reading the ARP table, a quick ping sweep, and reverse DNS.
    Read-only discovery: no passwords, no interception. Walk the list with the client and
    mark each device they account for as OWNED; anything left is unrecognized.
    Blocking is never automatic — you confirm each one, and it's logged.
    
    
    
      
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

    



    
  
    DROID CHECK what a phone is really running
    
  
    
    
    Plug the client's Android into this laptop by USB and read the phone's
    own configuration for the mechanisms screen-mirroring and stalkerware actually rely on —
    active screen capture, accessibility services, notification listeners, device admins,
    sideloaded apps and who holds mic/camera. Read-only: nothing on the phone is changed,
    no uninstall, no settings written.
    
    This does not say "clean" or "infected" — and no honest tool can. It ranks
    what to look at first and shows the evidence; you walk each item with the client.
